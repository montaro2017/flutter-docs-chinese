PopupMenuPositionBuilder typedef

PopupMenuPositionBuilder = RelativeRect Function(BuildContext context, BoxConstraints constraints)

用于创建 RelativeRect 以定位弹出菜单的构建器。BuildContext 和 BoxConstraints 均来自显示此菜单的 PopupRoute。

返回的 RelativeRect 决定弹出菜单相对于 Navigator 叠加层边界的位置。调用此回调时，菜单尺寸尚未确定，因为它取决于菜单项和其他属性。

RelativeRect 使用的坐标系以 Navigator 叠加层的左上角为原点。y 坐标正方向向下（位于原点下方），x 坐标正方向向右。

另请参阅：

  • RelativeRect.fromLTRB, which creates a RelativeRect from left, top, right, and bottom coordinates.
  • RelativeRect.fromRect, which creates a RelativeRect from two Rects, one representing the size of the popup menu and one representing the size of the overlay.
